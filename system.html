DAILYCARE / DESIGN FOUNDATIONS / V1.0

A system with a softer side.

Quiet foundations. Expressive little details. A consistent language that makes everyday care feel human.

01

Color, with a purpose

Paper--paper · #FBF8F2
Ink--ink · #303D32
Olive--olive · #3D4D37
Sage--sage · #E8EDDC
Peach--peach · #F3D8C5
Butter--butter · #F3E6AC
Lilac--lilac · #E2DCEF

Paper is the default canvas. Olive means action. Sage indicates recorded routine care. Butter highlights a change, without implying danger. Peach brings warmth to the family summary. State is always also conveyed through text or an icon.

Interaction, without the friction

06

Care in the details

Accessible by structure.
Native buttons, radios, checkboxes, and dialogs. Descriptive accessible names. Keyboard focus rings. Visible text alongside expressions and status. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Content scrolls above the persistent primary action.

Ready to implement.
All colors, typography, spacing, and radii are CSS custom properties in styles.css. The app uses plain HTML and a small vanilla script. Fonts and artwork are local assets. Component classes are shared by the app and this page.
